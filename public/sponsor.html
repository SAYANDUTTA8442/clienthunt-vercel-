FAQ

Common questions from sponsors

Can we sponsor a single event like ClientHunt rather than an annual tier?

Yes. Sponsors can partner for a specific event such as ClientHunt, back a single competition track (Web Development or Graphics Design), or support multiple initiatives across the academic term.

Do you accept non-monetary or in-kind sponsorships?

Yes. Cloud credits, developer APIs, design software licenses, domain vouchers, and participant merchandise are valuable ways to support student builders during sprints.

Can sponsors contribute a problem statement for participants?

Title and Track sponsors can work with our organizing team to frame a practical problem statement or case study for participants, subject to academic and competition fairness guidelines.

How are sponsorships administered?

All partnerships are coordinated transparently under Wings of Arthniti, STC, IIT Patna (Hybrid) with agreed deliverables documented prior to event announcements.
