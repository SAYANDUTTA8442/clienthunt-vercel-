ClientHunt Registration at IIT Patna.

Register individually or with a team of up to four eligible IIT Patna students for ClientHunt, a business problem-solving and product development competition organised by Freelanthropy. Analyse a real-world business problem, develop a practical solution, and execute your ideas.

  • IIT Patna Internal Event
  • Team Size: 1-4
  • Two-Round Competition
