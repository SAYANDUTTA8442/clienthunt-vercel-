About

Learning that continues into practice.

Freelanthropy brings students together to explore professional opportunities, build practical capabilities and work on meaningful challenges, individually and in teams.

We connect students with peers, mentors and potential collaborators, so that skills developed in the classroom can be applied in practical settings.

Mission

To create opportunities for students to develop professional capabilities through practical learning, collaborative projects and industry-oriented engagement.

Vision

To foster a collaborative ecosystem where students can transform their skills and ideas into meaningful professional outcomes.

FAQ

Common questions

What is Freelanthropy?

Freelanthropy is a student-led initiative under Wings of Arthniti, which operates under STC, IIT Patna (Hybrid). It focuses on freelancing, professional skill development, collaboration and entrepreneurship.

What is ClientHunt?

ClientHunt is an event organised by Freelanthropy, with a separate page of its own. Round 1 is an online qualifier built around a common problem statement, with an Ideathon, a Case Study and Professional Mail Writing, followed by evaluation and shortlisting. Round 2 is a three-hour offline Product Development Sprint.

Who can participate in ClientHunt?

Teams of 1 to 4 participants. See the ClientHunt page for current details.

How is Round 2 evaluated?

The relevant Web Development and Graphics Design departments evaluate their respective submissions independently. The Organizing Team then consolidates the scores and publishes the final results.

How can I get involved?

Students can explore membership on the Join page. Companies, professionals and mentors can use the Collaborate page, and organisations can reach us through the Sponsor page.

How do I contact the team?

Email freelanthropy.iitpatna@gmail.com.
