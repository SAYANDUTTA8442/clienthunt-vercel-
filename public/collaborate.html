FAQ

Questions from partners & mentors

Who executes client and collaborative projects?

Projects are executed by vetted student teams from IIT Patna (Hybrid) across engineering, design, and operations, overseen by Freelanthropy coordinators to ensure clear communication and timely handoff.

What kind of mentorship commitment is expected?

Mentorship is flexible. You can host a single 60-minute workshop, review student project architectures asynchronously, or mentor a team during an active sprint like ClientHunt.

How are project scopes and IP handled?

Before work begins, both parties align on written deliverables, timelines, and ownership terms during the scoping call so expectations remain transparent throughout the engagement.

Can early-stage startups collaborate with Freelanthropy?

Yes. Startups, founders, research groups, and established organisations are all welcome to propose MVPs, design sprints, research tasks, or problem statements.
